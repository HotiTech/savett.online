[**SaveTT.online**](https://savett.online/) is a free, high-speed online utility designed to download TikTok media directly in clean MP4 (watermark-free) and high-bitrate MP3 audio formats. No third-party software installation or account registration required.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **No Watermark Export:** Grab clean TikTok videos in full resolution (up to 1080p HD).
- **MP3 Audio Extraction:** Convert TikTok sounds and background tracks into high-quality 320kbps MP3 files.
- **Bulk & Batch Downloads:** Download multiple links or full video sets without manually opening every single tab.
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Runs directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS.
- **Privacy-Friendly:** No login credentials, cookies, or personal tracking needed.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Copy Link:** Copy any public video URL from the TikTok app or web client.
2. **Paste & Fetch:** Visit [savett.online](https://savett.online/), paste your link into the search box, and click **Fetch / Search**.
3. **Choose & Download:** Select your desired format (**MP4 No Watermark** or **MP3**) and save directly to your storage.
